# Tuneferry privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Tuneferry moves playlists between Spotify and YouTube inside your own browser.

## What it uses
- Playlist and song information from the pages you ask it to read.
- Your existing Spotify and YouTube sessions in this browser, only to search songs and to create playlists in your own account when you press Create or Save.

## What it stores
- Search results, kept on your device for 7 days to make repeat searches faster.
- Your current list, so you can continue after closing the tab.

You can clear saved searches in Settings. Removing the extension deletes everything.

## What it never does
- It never sends your data, login, cookies or tokens to the developer or any third party. There is no server.
- It never sells or shares data.
- It never uses data for ads, profiling or credit decisions.
- It only acts on Spotify and YouTube when you ask it to.
